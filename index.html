Methodology

Analysis covers January 2019–July 2026. October and November 2025 inflation regimes are missing because the October CPI observation was unavailable, preventing both monthly inflation calculations. Regime analysis is descriptive, not predictive. Twelve-month rolling correlation requires at least 12 months of observations.

Monthly rebalanced portfolio; no trading costs. Daily levels use the last valid monthly observation; FEDFUNDS is a monthly average. Rising/falling and widening/tightening use the sign of monthly changes; zero changes are neutral. High inflation and VIX exceed their sample medians (0.2842% monthly inflation; VIX 17.52). Volatility uses sample standard deviation × √12. Unusually high correlation exceeds the full-sample correlation plus one rolling-correlation standard deviation (0.7203). No missing values are filled.
